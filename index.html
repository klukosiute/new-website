I am a program officer at the OpenAI Foundation, where I am building the cyber resilience workstream.

I care deeply about making sure AI benefits all of humanity. Forty years of advances—in medicine, in the power grid, in the financial system—were built on assumptions of trust and determinism in computing. AI is rapidly upending that trust, and we must figure out the set of controls that allows us to trust computing again at a fundamental level.
